# Glossary

## Public surface

The site's visitor-facing routes: home, institutional pages, category and tag
archives, posts, and RSS.

## New-surface phase

The first migration stage, delivering the Astro home and institutional pages
before importing the blog archive.

## Blog archive

The 38 Portuguese posts currently published by Hugo, with their metadata, URLs,
and media references retained. Migrating this archive is the final stage.

## psql session

The visual, interactive home-page emulator that presents professional data
through predefined commands and their results.
